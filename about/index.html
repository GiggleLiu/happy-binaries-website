Jin-Guo Liu (刘金国)

Computational physicist · Open-source enthusiast

Assistant professor at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), Advanced Materials Thrust and the Quantum Science and Technology Center. PhD from Nanjing University (2017); postdoc at the Institute of Physics (CAS) and Harvard University. I love the open-source community and believe in making science accessible through code — I am one of the main authors of the quantum simulator Yao.jl.
