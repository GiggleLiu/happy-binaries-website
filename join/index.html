Join us

We are recruiting PhD students and research assistants in quantum many-body computation and quantum computation, jointly with Prof. Xi Dai's group at HKUST (Clear Water Bay). Applications are reviewed year-round.

What you will learn

  • World-class scientific computing. Not boring engineering practice — training in computational complexity, mathematics, and algorithms, building on our open-source stack (Yao.jl, OMEinsum, GenericTensorNetworks.jl).
  • Quantum algorithm design, including algorithms for neutral-atom (Rydberg) systems — experience from the Lukin group at Harvard.
  • Computational complexity theory — one of the ultimate questions statistical physics hopes to explore.
  • AI-native research workflows. We treat human-AI collaboration as a first-class research skill: test-driven vibe coding, agent automation, reproducible software. See our blog.
  • Collaboration and travel — long-term visits to Clear Water Bay, and strong support for attending international conferences.

How we work

  • Topic-based discussion on HKUST-GZ Zulip; a WeChat group for lunch and coffee.
  • All code on GitHub — CodingThrust — mostly in Julia.
  • Papers shared via Zotero.
  • Live coding with Jin-Guo every Friday, 20:00 GMT+8 (calendar).

Required reading

  • Nielsen & Chuang, Quantum Computation and Quantum Information.
  • Moore & Mertens, The Nature of Computation.
  • Golub & Van Loan, Matrix Computations.

More training material: our book Scientific Computing for Physicists, the CodingClub repo (git, shell, cluster, MPI), and MIT's Missing Semester.

Requirements

  • Integrity and a cooperative personality — willing to consult others and to help.
  • Quantum mechanics (required); quantum computing is a plus.
  • Pluses: Linux, git, a programming language (Julia recommended), data structures.
  • PhD applicants need a bachelor's degree and English proficiency per HKUST(GZ) admission rules (TOEFL ≥ 80 or IELTS ≥ 6.5, unless your prior degree was taught in English).
  • RA positions suit bachelor's graduates preparing a PhD application (a common on-ramp — RAs can apply for the PhD program while working with us).

How to apply

  1. Email jinguoliu@hkust-gz.edu.cn with your CV, transcript, and any publications. Subject line: position + your name.
  2. Suitable candidates are interviewed by group members about background, skills, and research interests.
  3. Openings remain valid until filled.

Curious about the campus? Video tour — maker space, gym, pool, and a river across campus.
